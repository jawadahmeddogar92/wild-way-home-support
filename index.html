Wild Way Home — Support

Welcome! Wild Way Home is a cozy logic puzzle game where you help a lost girl find her way home through a jungle by solving puzzles to reveal hidden animals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do hints work?
Tap the hint button to get a nudge toward the next correct move. Reveals will place a piece for you directly. Both are limited — watch a short ad to refill them.

I lost all my hearts — what happens?
You can watch a short ad for an extra heart and continue the same level, or restart the level.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Open the settings gear icon in the top bar during gameplay.

I found a bug or the app crashed — what do I do?
Please email us below with your device model and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot!
