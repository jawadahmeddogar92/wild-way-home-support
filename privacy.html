Wild Way Home — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ARRK Studio ("we," "us") handles information in connection with the Wild Way Home mobile app (the "App").

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address directly.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as your advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage and interaction data (such as ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostic and performance data (such as crash logs)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, you will be asked for permission before the App or its advertising partners track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this choice anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

Locally Stored Data

The App stores your game progress, settings, and hint/reveal counts locally on your device (via standard iOS storage). This data is not transmitted to us and is removed if you uninstall the App.

Children's Privacy

Wild Way Home is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising shown in the App is served through Google AdMob's standard settings; we do not target ads specifically at children under 13.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected via AdMob is shared with Google and its advertising partners solely for the purpose of delivering and measuring ads, as described above.

Your Choices

You can limit ad tracking through your device's privacy settings, or opt out of personalized advertising where supported by your device and region.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
